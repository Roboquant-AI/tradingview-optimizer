The missing optimization layer for TradingView. Free and open source.

TradingView gives you Pine Script. It gives you backtesting. What it doesn't give you is a way to systematically find the best parameter settings for your strategy.

You're left clicking through values one at a time, eyeballing results, and hoping you land somewhere good. That's not optimization. That's guessing.

This extension fixes that. No account, no login, no paid tier.

WHAT IT DOES

Select any strategy inputs, define a search range, and run a full grid search on your own chart. Every combination runs through TradingView's Strategy Tester. No external simulator, no data export or import on your side.

When the optimization finishes, you get a sortable results table ranked by your chosen goal. Found the best settings? Apply them to your chart with one click.


HOW IT WORKS

1. Open any TradingView chart with a Pine Script strategy applied
2. Click Optimizer in the panel tab bar below the chart
3. Your strategy inputs are detected automatically, no setup needed
4. Select the inputs you want to optimize and set min / max / step
5. Choose your goal: Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, net profit %, profit factor, win rate or minimum drawdown
6. Hit Start and watch results come in as each combination is tested
7. Sort and compare results in the built-in table
8. Apply the parameter set you want to your chart with one click


OPTIMIZATION MODES

Standard Mode
Grid search across your selected inputs on the current chart. The fastest way to find better settings for any strategy.

Multi-Timeframe Mode
Test your strategy across multiple timeframes in a single run. A strategy that only works on the 1H chart isn't a strategy, it's a coincidence. Multi-timeframe testing shows whether your edge holds across time horizons.

Multi-Symbol Mode
Validate your settings across different instruments without switching charts by hand. One run, many symbols.

Full Grid Mode
Exhaustive sweep of every combination of inputs, symbols and timeframes together. Thorough, systematic and complete.


WALK-FORWARD ANALYSIS

Optimized parameters that fall apart on new data are worse than no optimization at all. Walk-forward analysis is the standard way to detect overfitting.

Split your history into an in-sample period, where inputs are optimized, and an out-of-sample period, where they are checked on data the optimizer never saw. If the settings hold up out-of-sample, you have evidence of a real edge rather than a curve fit.

Single Split
One in-sample and one out-of-sample window. Quick validation for any parameter set.

Rolling Walk-Forward
Slides the in-sample / out-of-sample windows across your date range in configurable steps (window sizes and step in months). This simulates re-optimizing periodically, the most realistic test of whether your strategy adapts or decays.


PARAMETER HEATMAPS

Numbers tell you what happened. Heatmaps show you why.

2D Heatmaps
Pick two inputs and a metric. Every combination is colored so you can instantly see which zones are consistently good and which are fragile one-off peaks. A robust strategy shows broad stable regions, not isolated hot spots.

3D Surface Plots
Rotate, zoom and hover across the full parameter landscape to see how your inputs interact.

Metrics: Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, net profit %, profit factor, win rate, max drawdown or total trades.


PROP FIRM CHALLENGE CHECK

Check your best result against a profit target, a daily loss limit and a maximum drawdown for a given account size before you take a challenge.


RESULTS, APPLY AND HISTORY

- Sortable table with net profit %, Sharpe ratio, profit factor, win rate, max drawdown and trade count
- One-click Apply for the best result or any row
- Download results as JSON for analysis in Python, R, Excel or any other tool
- Your last 10 optimizations are saved locally so you can review them without re-running

Metrics are calculated from TradingView's exported trade list for each run, so every combination is scored the same way.
